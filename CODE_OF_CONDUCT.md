# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Tl;dr: be respectful, assume good faith, and don't be a jerk. If someone's
behaviour is making the community worse, report it via GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/novucs/metabase-sync/security/advisories/new)
on this repo (it's repurposed for conduct issues too — the form is private and
goes only to the maintainer), or send a GitHub DM to
[@novucs](https://github.com/novucs).

The full text:

> ### Our pledge
>
> We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
> community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
> size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
> identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
> status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
> sexual identity and orientation.
>
> We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
> diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

For the full text of the standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and
attribution, see <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
